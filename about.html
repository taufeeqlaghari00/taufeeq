TAUFEEQ LAGHARIPAKISTAN

I’m a WordPress Developer & CMS Specialist with 1.6 years of hands-on experience building and improving websites.

My work spans WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, and CMS development. I enjoy the point where design and practical implementation meet: translating a layout into a responsive page, organizing content so it is simple to maintain, and making sure the experience holds together on smaller screens.

I believe a good website should be more than a visual exercise. It should explain what a business does, make important information easy to find, and help visitors take the next step with confidence.

That means asking useful questions early, paying attention to spacing and type, testing real interactions, and keeping the code as clear as the project allows.
